**Hello! My name is Vitor Casadei.**
I am an Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Specialist with over 10 years of experience working at the forefront of technology and innovation. Currently, I am a *Senior Data Science Manager* at CESAR and a Postgraduate Professor of AI, Deep Learning, and Data Science at CESAR School.

## My Journey and Purpose
I hold a Master's degree from UFSCar, and my true passion is using technology as a driver for social inclusion. Throughout my career, I've had the privilege of leading high-performance teams in developing solutions that impact people's lives. I am very proud to have been part of the technical leadership for pioneering accessibility projects, such as the world's first AI-based simultaneous sign language (Libras) translator (a partnership between Lenovo and CESAR) and an intelligent lipstick for people with motor and visual impairments (with Grupo Boticário). I strongly believe in the premise of "AI for All" and how computer vision and interface design can break down barriers.

## What you will find in this space
I created this blog to share my thoughts, learnings, and daily experiences with technology. Here, I write about:

* **Artificial Intelligence and Accessibility:** Real-world use cases and how technology transforms lives.
* **Applied Research and Management:** Tips on agile methodologies in R&D, such as adapting Scrum for applied research (*Scrum for Applied Research*).
* **Software Architecture and Data Science:** Reflections on the field, best practices, and technical leadership.
* **Personal Projects and *Hands-on* Work:** I enjoy sharing my IoT and infrastructure projects — from applying AI and object detection to home security cameras (*Frigate*) to upgrading networks in rural areas, as well as my adventures in the 3D printing world.

If you want to chat about AI, innovation, accessibility, or just exchange ideas about technology, feel free to connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://br.linkedin.com/in/vcasadei) or send me an email at [vitor.casadei2@gmail.com](mailto:vitor.casadei2@gmail.com).
